## 2. Teleprompter for Video: short scripts and editing

**Consider it for:** a brief talking video you want to caption or reframe on the phone.

The developer documents recording, saving/sharing, trimming and resizing in its free tier, with a 750-character script limit. Its App Store description explicitly says these short recordings have no watermark. That makes it a useful first candidate for a short introduction or update. Count characters, including spaces, rather than assuming every one-minute script will fit. Free-tier details, App Store description.

The iPhone listing also advertises voice-assisted scrolling. We have not tested its tracking accuracy, microphone behavior or recovery after an ad-lib. Treat that as a feature to try with your own voice, not a guarantee of a clean take. iPhone feature listing.

**Tradeoff:** longer scripts require an upgrade. The support guide and store expose different price options, so use the purchase confirmation on your phone for the actual offer. This is not a verified current one-time-purchase recommendation.

## Test your shortlist with the same script

A readable prompt is only part of the job. Check the saved video and what happens when you make a mistake. Use this same short script in each app, with the same phone position, lighting and microphone:

> This is a short camera test. I am reading from the phone while keeping my attention close to the lens. I will pause here for a breath. Then I will continue at a comfortable pace. If I lose my place, I will stop and restart this sentence. At the end I want a saved video I can find, play and share. Before I record the real version, I will check the sound, the framing and whether the script appears in the exported picture.

The sample is **83 words and 423 characters**, including spaces. At 140 words per minute, the words alone take about **36 seconds**; pauses add time. You can adapt it using the [speech-time calculator](/tools/speech-time-calculator/). Do not change the text between apps when you want to compare a free script limit.

1. Paste the sample into a new script. Note any account, import or payment step before recording.
2. Adjust the text from your filming distance. Record one continuous take, including the pause and a deliberate restart.
3. For speech following, add an unscripted sentence and return to the next written line. Record what actually happens.
4. Find the take, trim the beginning if available, then save or export it. Play the exported file outside the app.
5. Note the watermark, dimensions, sound, crop and any paywall. Keep the app version and purchase tier beside the result.

Once an app passes those checks, [practice reading naturally on iPhone](/guides/read-teleprompter-naturally-iphone/) with the same setup. Work on the delivery before recording the full script.

Choose the app that handles your real script and recovery steps.
